  A research project optimizing visual symbol sets for patients with retinal implants. By analyzing confusion
  matrices over simulated letter recognition using the pulse2percept framework, SymbolSight derives symbol sets
  that remain distinguishable even under the severe distortions introduced by low-resolution prosthetic vision.
