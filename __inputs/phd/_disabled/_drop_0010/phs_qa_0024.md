## **Statement of Purpose (SOP) – “Why grad school, why this program, why you”**

UCSB’s official prompt: your SOP should give **“a brief statement outlining your reasons for undertaking a graduate program, your particular area of specialization within the major field, your past academic work, and your plans for future occupation or profession,”** plus anything that helps evaluate your preparation and aptitude for grad study at UCSB.([Graduate Division](https://www.graddiv.ucsb.edu/admissions/how-complete-online-application))  
 It must be in **narrative essay form** and **must not duplicate** your Personal History Statement.([Graduate Division](https://www.graddiv.ucsb.edu/admissions/how-complete-online-application))

So for a CS PhD, your SOP should cover:

### **A. Your academic & research goals**

* Why you want a **graduate degree in computer science**, and specifically a **PhD**.

* The **research area(s)** you’re interested in (e.g., systems, ML, theory, HCI, security, graphics, etc.).

* The big questions or problems you want to work on.

*(This addresses “reasons for undertaking a graduate program” and “area of specialization within the major field.”)([Graduate Division](https://www.graddiv.ucsb.edu/admissions/how-complete-online-application))*

### **B. Your preparation**

* Key **courses** that prepared you for research (advanced algorithms, operating systems, ML, etc.).

* Significant **research experiences**: projects, undergrad thesis, RA positions, internships with a research component.

* What you actually **did** (methods, tools, contributions) and what came out of it (papers, posters, code, systems, negative results—anything that shows serious engagement).

* Any other academic work that shows you’re ready for **rigorous, independent research** (e.g., math or statistics background, interdisciplinary work).

*(This matches the prompt’s emphasis on “past academic work” and information that helps evaluate your preparation and aptitude.)([Graduate Division](https://www.graddiv.ucsb.edu/admissions/how-complete-online-application))*

### **C. Your future plans**

* Your **long-term direction**: faculty, research lab, industry R\&D, startup, etc.

* How a **UCSB CS PhD** specifically fits those plans and helps you get there.

*(This covers “plans for future occupation or profession.”)([Graduate Division](https://www.graddiv.ucsb.edu/admissions/how-complete-online-application))*

### **D. Why UCSB CS specifically**

While the general prompt is the same for all departments, the CS program page confirms you must submit this SOP and directs you to follow the Grad Division prompts.([Graduate Division](https://www.graddiv.ucsb.edu/graduate-programs/departments/computer-science))  
 So you should also:

* Mention **specific faculty** whose work aligns with your interests and why (but don’t just name-drop; connect to your past work and goals).

* Reference **relevant labs/groups** or areas at UCSB (e.g., networking, ML, architecture, systems, HCI, security, etc. as listed on the CS grad page).([Graduate Division](https://www.graddiv.ucsb.edu/graduate-programs/departments/computer-science))

* Explain why UCSB’s environment (interdisciplinary opportunities, particular research strengths) is a good fit for your goals.

### **E. Any additional information relevant to your academic profile**

UCSB explicitly invites **“any additional information”** that helps them evaluate your preparation and aptitude.([Graduate Division](https://www.graddiv.ucsb.edu/admissions/how-complete-online-application)) This might include:

* Brief context for anomalies (e.g., one bad term, school disruptions) **only if** it’s relevant and you haven’t covered it in the PHS.

* Unique technical experiences (open-source work, competitive programming, major software you’ve built) that show research/CS maturity.

👉 **Key constraint:** Keep this essay focused on your *academic and research trajectory*; save most of your personal background, hardships, and diversity contributions for the **Personal History Statement**, to avoid duplication.

**SOP:**

* Why a **CS PhD** and what subfield(s)?

* Main **research questions/interests**.

* Detailed **research experiences** and your contributions.

* Relevant **coursework/skills** (theoretical, systems, ML, etc.).

* **Future career goals** and how UCSB CS specifically fits.

* Names of **potential advisors** and how you align with them.

* Any extra info that helps evaluate your **preparation/aptitude**.
